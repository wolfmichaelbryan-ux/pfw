Rasheed Bello
EuroLeague · Virtus Bologna (Italy)
Signed a multi-year deal with EuroLeague power Virtus Bologna in 2026 — the highest level of basketball outside the NBA. Two years earlier he was leading the Horizon League in steals and earning First Team All-League in a Mastodon uniform.
Jarred Godfrey
Pro · BG Göttingen (BBL, Germany)
Our all-time leading scorer (2,164 points) and a three-time All-Horizon League selection. Broke into the pros putting up 27 a night in Sweden, and now plays for BG Göttingen in Germany's Basketball Bundesliga. Top-3 in program history in rebounds, threes, steals, assists AND free-throw percentage.
Bryson Scott
G-League Draft · Toronto Raptors Org · Europe
Fort Wayne kid. 9th in the nation in scoring (22.2 PPG), single-season program scoring record (746 points), All-Summit First Team, and a selection to the NABC Reese's College All-Star Game at the Final Four. Taken by Toronto in the 2018 G-League Draft — one of five Mastodons drafted since 2013.
Max Landis
Pro · Belgium, Germany, Portugal
2016 Summit League Player of the Year, Lou Henson All-American, and #2 in the entire NCAA in 3PT% — behind only Buddy Hield. Worked out for the Pacers, Bucks, Bulls and Jazz before a pro career across Europe.
Frank Gaines
G-League · Italy, Poland, Russia, Spain
Dropped 22.4 PPG for the Celtics' G-League affiliate — 4th in the entire league — shooting 40% from three, and took home D-League Rookie of the Year AND Most Improved. Played in Italy's first division and ran NBA Summer League with the Pacers, Heat and Thunder.
